One private family workspace

Less chasing. Less reconstructing. One organized record.

Kinbriva brings the everyday pieces of family coordination together without requiring a second account, shared login or cooperative co-parent.

01 / CAPTURE

Keep the source.

Scan or import documents, save notes separately, and preserve the original in the encrypted local vault.

02 / COORDINATE

Keep everyday parenting organized.

Use Calendar, the solo communication log, Exchange Log, Parenting Log, Handoff Brief, Medication Center and structured schedule-change requests.

03 / PREPARE

Build the Casebook.

Search a unified timeline, filter for evidence or follow-up, and export a focused case report when needed.

Private by default. Core records stay in an encrypted device vault and remain available offline after unlock. Live mailbox access, automatic child-support portal synchronization and cloud sharing are not active in 0.2.0.

Kinbriva 0.2.0 is being prepared as the next update, including adaptive layouts for iPhone Duo. Kinbriva organizes information and review prompts; it does not determine legal compliance, provide medical advice or file court documents.
