A subscription is required to set up a family and create or update records, payments, schedules and entered awards. There is no free tier or free trial.

KINBRIVA PLUS

$4.99 per month (US)

The monthly plan includes unlimited original imports, authorized visit recording, supported local transcription, and on-device summary and reply drafts. Files remain limited to 25 MB and documents to 100 pages.

YOUR CHOICE

Clear renewal terms.

A required monthly subscription billed by Apple, renewing automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Apple Account > Subscriptions. The app shows the current localized price before purchase. After access ends, existing records stay readable and exportable; backups, restore and deletion remain available.

The app and subscription are being prepared for Apple review and are not publicly available yet. AI drafts require iOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence-capable hardware and an available local model. Local transcription requires available on-device speech recognition. Read plan support, the privacy policy and Apple Standard EULA.
