Kinbriva / Privacy

Privacy starts with
the working record.

This policy explains the local iPhone and iPad version of Kinbriva and the support website.

Applies to Kinbriva 0.2.0 · Effective November 5, 2026
Owner: Heather Berg / Innexis
Contact: heather.knapp83@gmail.com

The app does not send family records to a developer-operated record-storage server. Sharing, exporting or saving an item to another app is an action chosen by the person using the device.

Information kept on the device

Kinbriva can store information entered or imported by the device user, including children’s names, optional birthdays, school and activity details, parenting arrangements, court orders, receipts, financial records, appointments, communication-log entries and reply follow-up status, school and activity information, Parenting Log entries, schedule-change requests, Exchange Log observations such as entered location, arrival/departure times, outcome and witness, medication profiles and recorded doses, private notes, original documents, photos and permitted recordings.

Workspace data and original files are encrypted on the iPhone or iPad. The vault key is stored in the device’s passcode-protected Keychain. Device authentication is required to unlock the workspace. Core records remain available offline after the vault is unlocked. Adding another parent’s name does not invite that person or grant access.

On-device processing

Document recognition uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. Supported devices may use Apple’s local language model for summary drafts and communication assistance. Transcription is requested only when on-device speech recognition is available. Kinbriva does not fall back to uploading audio for transcription.

OCR, transcripts and generated text can contain errors. Review them against the complete original before relying on them. Kinbriva includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Permissions

Kinbriva may request camera access for document scanning, microphone access for permitted recordings, speech-recognition access for local transcription and device authentication for the encrypted vault. Exchange Log location is entered by the user or copied from a selected Kinbriva calendar event; version 0.2.0 does not silently collect background location. A device permission does not establish that recording is lawful; the device user is responsible for permissions required for each participant and location.

When a selected Kinbriva event is sent to Apple Calendar, Kinbriva opens Apple’s system event editor so the user can review and save the event. Kinbriva does not request broad read access to the device calendar. Apple’s editor may offer contact-based invitees; if the user chooses that feature, iOS may request Contacts permission. Kinbriva does not independently read, upload or synchronize the contact list.

Apple Calendar and external destinations

Saving an event in Apple Calendar creates a separate copy controlled by Apple Calendar and the user’s selected calendar account. Kinbriva does not read changes back from the calendar. Files, case reports, handoff briefs and encrypted backups shared to another app or person are governed by that destination’s practices. Exported originals are not automatically redacted.

Local reminders

If notification permission is allowed, Kinbriva can schedule local reminders on the device for upcoming family events, expense due dates, medication refills/expiration and saved child birthdays. Generic notification text is the default. Item names are shown only if the user enables that option. Version 0.2.0 does not use a developer-operated push-notification server for these family-record reminders.

Email and child-support portals

Version 0.2.0 does not connect to a mailbox, synchronize child-support portals or collect portal credentials. A parent can import selected EML or PDF files, paste email text or import a reviewed payment-template CSV. Official resource links open external websites.

Any later live mailbox, portal, cloud-sharing or shared-account connection will require a policy update and explicit account consent.

Backups, retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until the local workspace is deleted or the app is removed. Standard automatic device backup of the vault is excluded. Kinbriva can create an encrypted portable backup protected by a separate passphrase. Keep that passphrase separately; it cannot be recovered by the developer.

Local workspace deletion removes local vault files and the device vault key. It does not remove backups, exports, Apple Calendar copies, support emails or copies saved by other people or apps.

Apple purchases and subscriptions

New Kinbriva Plus purchases use an auto-renewing annual subscription billed by Apple. The planned US price for version 0.2.0 is $10.99 per year, with no free tier or free trial. StoreKit supplies Apple-signed transaction and renewal information so the app can determine access on the device. Kinbriva does not send receipts, transaction identifiers or purchase history to a developer-operated receipt server, and the developer does not receive payment card details.

Existing users with an active legacy monthly Kinbriva Plus entitlement continue to receive Plus access while that Apple-signed entitlement remains active. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account > Subscriptions. After a paid entitlement ends, existing records remain readable, searchable and exportable; encrypted backup, restore and local deletion remain available. New entries and changes require active Plus access.

Children

Kinbriva is intended for adults managing family records. It does not provide child accounts, advertising profiles or public child profiles in this build.

Support and this website

This website has no family-record upload form, advertising script or analytics script. Its hosting provider may process standard request information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the pages.

If a person emails support, the sender’s address and message contents are received through the support email provider and used to respond to the request. Avoid sending private family documents, recording files, passwords or backup passphrases.

Policy changes and questions

This policy will be reviewed whenever data handling changes, including mailbox access, cloud synchronization, shared accounts, analytics, remote AI, developer-hosted purchase processing or support uploads.
