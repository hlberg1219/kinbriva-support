Kinbriva / Privacy

Privacy starts with
the working record.

This policy explains the local iPhone and iPad version of Kinbriva and the support website.

Applies to Kinbriva 0.1.0 · Effective November 1, 2026
Owner: Heather Berg / Innexis
Contact: heather.knapp83@gmail.com

The local app does not send family records to a developer-operated record-storage server. Sharing an export is a separate action chosen by the person using the device.

Information kept on the device

Kinbriva can store information entered or imported by the device user, including children’s names, school and activity details, parenting arrangements, court orders, receipts, financial records, appointments, notes, communications and permitted recordings.

Workspace data and original files are encrypted on the iPhone or iPad. The vault key is stored in the device’s passcode-protected Keychain. Device authentication is required to unlock the workspace. Adding another parent’s name does not invite that person or grant access to records.

Document and recording processing

Document recognition uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. Supported devices may use Apple’s local language model for summary drafts and communication assistance. Transcription is requested only when on-device speech recognition is available. The app does not fall back to uploading audio for transcription.

OCR, transcripts and generated text can contain errors. Review them against the complete original before relying on them. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Device permissions

The app requests camera access when scanning documents, microphone access when recording a visit, speech recognition permission when creating a local transcript, and device authentication when unlocking records.

A device permission does not establish that a particular conversation may lawfully be recorded. Obtain the permissions required for the visit, provider and participant locations before recording.

Exports and sharing

The device user can save or share selected records and encrypted backups. An export sent to another app, storage service, agency, professional or person is governed by that destination’s practices. Other apps may keep copies outside Kinbriva.

Exported originals are not automatically redacted. Using initials for profile labels does not remove names or other private information inside documents, quotations or free text. Review every exported file before sharing it.

Email and child-support portals

Version 0.1.0 does not connect to a mailbox, synchronize child-support portals or collect portal credentials. A parent can import selected EML or PDF files, paste email text, or import a reviewed payment-template CSV. Official resource links open external websites.

Any later live connection will require a policy update and explicit consent to the account access involved.

Retention, backups and deletion

Records remain on the device until the local workspace is deleted or the app is removed. Automatic device backup of the vault is excluded. Use the app’s encrypted portable backup to preserve records and original files, and keep its passphrase separately.

Losing the device or passphrase without a usable backup may prevent recovery. Deleting the local workspace removes its vault files and device vault key. It does not remove exports or backups saved elsewhere, or copies received by other people. Corrections append history while originals remain in the workspace until deletion.

Apple purchases and subscriptions

An auto-renewing Kinbriva monthly subscription, billed by Apple, is required to create and update records. The US price is $4.99 per month, with no free tier or free trial. StoreKit supplies Apple-signed transaction and renewal information to the app so it can determine access on the device. The app does not send receipts, transaction identifiers or purchase history to a developer-operated server, and the developer does not receive your payment card details.

Apple processes purchases under its own privacy terms and may provide sales and subscription reports to the developer. See Apple’s App Store privacy information. Restore purchases uses the Apple Account signed in to the App Store; it restores subscription access, not family records. Records are restored separately through an encrypted backup.

Manage or cancel in Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app or local workspace does not cancel a subscription. Cancellation preserves access until the paid period ends. After expiration or refund, existing records remain readable and exportable; encrypted backups, restore and deletion remain available. New family setup, entries, changes, imports, recordings, transcription and AI drafts require an active subscription. An import or recording started while subscribed can finish saving.

Children’s information

Kinbriva is intended for adults managing family records. This version provides no child accounts, advertising profiles or public child profiles.

Support and this website

This website has no record-upload form, advertising script or analytics script. It is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the pages.

If a person emails support, the sender’s email address and the information included in the message are received through the support email provider. That information is used to answer the request and maintain a support record, and is not used for advertising. Support messages remain in the support mailbox until deleted. Avoid sending private family documents, passwords or backup passphrases. Contact the address above to request deletion of a support message; the request is subject to the email provider’s retention and recovery processes.

Policy changes and questions

This policy will be reviewed when data handling changes, including mailbox access, cloud synchronization, new purchase-data handling, analytics, support uploads or shared accounts. The policy provided with a later version must describe that version’s actual practices.
