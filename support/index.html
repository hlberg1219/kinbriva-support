A little help
with the details.

These instructions describe Kinbriva 0.2.0, including the redesigned Today, Calendar, Messages, Records and Casebook experience.

Start with the family record.

  1. Set a device passcode, then unlock Kinbriva with device authentication.
  2. Review Kinbriva Plus. New subscriptions are planned at $10.99 per year through Apple, with no free tier or free trial.
  3. Add the applicable parenting order and covered children.
  4. Use the global Add control to capture communication, Exchange Log entries, records, events, expenses, payments, Parenting Log entries, medications or incidents.
  5. Review original files and extracted information, then create an encrypted backup.
What does Kinbriva Plus cost?

Version 0.2.0 is configured for a $10.99/year US auto-renewing subscription through Apple. The localized App Store price shown in the app controls the actual purchase. Existing active legacy monthly subscriptions continue to unlock Plus while Apple reports that entitlement as active.

How do I add a receipt, document or incident?

Use Add from any primary tab. Choose Receipt, Incident, Document or other record, then select the parenting order, child, type and event date. Imported originals are encrypted in the local vault. Files are limited to 25 MB and PDF imports to 100 pages.

What is Parenting Log?

Parenting Log stores day-to-day information such as medication, meals, sleep, mood, health, school, activities, behavior, packing and private notes. Entries remain private until deliberately shared or included in an export.

What is Handoff Brief?

Handoff Brief prepares a reviewable summary from the latest Parenting Log information and upcoming calendar items. Review the preview before sharing. Creating a brief does not automatically send anything to another parent.

How do schedule-change requests work?

Open Calendar and choose Request schedule change on an event. Kinbriva preserves the original event and stores the requested dates, reason, response, timestamp and outcome separately. Recording an outcome does not send a message automatically.

How does Messages work if the other parent does not use Kinbriva?

Messages is a solo-first private communication log. Record received, sent or draft communication, mark received items that need a reply, search the history and link an entry to source evidence, a calendar event or an expense. Saving another parent’s name does not invite that person or grant access. Kinbriva does not automatically send a communication.

What is Exchange Log?

Exchange Log records what was observed at a pickup or drop-off, including the effective planned time, actual arrival and optional departure, entered location, outcome, witness, factual notes and linked evidence. If a schedule change was accepted, the log preserves the approved effective time while retaining the original calendar event. Version 0.2.0 does not silently record audio, video or background location.

How do expenses and payments work?

Expenses can include payee/provider, split percentage, recurring status, third-party payment context, supporting records and requested, accepted, declined, disputed or paid status. Decision or rejection notes remain linked to the original expense. Payments can be partial; Kinbriva does not replace an agency-certified balance.

How does Medication Center work?

Medication Center stores the medication name, dosage, label or clinician instructions, prescriber, pharmacy, refill and expiration dates, active/archive status and recorded dose history. It is a recordkeeping tool and does not recommend a dose or replace the prescribing clinician or medication label.

Can Kinbriva add events to Apple Calendar?

Yes. Choose Add to Apple Calendar from a Kinbriva event. Apple’s system event editor opens so you can review and save the event. Kinbriva does not request broad read access to the device calendar or synchronize changes back from Calendar.

How do local reminders work?

If notification permission is allowed, Kinbriva can schedule on-device reminders for upcoming family events, expense due dates, medication refills and medication expiration. Generic lock-screen text is the default; item names appear only if that option is enabled in Kinbriva Notifications settings. Version 0.2.0 does not use a developer push-notification server for these family-record reminders.

How do I use an order when reviewing a message?

Import the complete order under Records and verify extracted pages against the original. From Casebook, open Family settings and advanced tools, then Confirmed order clauses. Save only a reviewed exact quotation. Message assistance produces an editable draft and does not determine legal compliance or send a message automatically.

Are Gmail, Outlook or support portals connected?

No live mailbox search or automatic child-support portal synchronization is active in version 0.2.0. Import selected emails or reviewed payment files and use official agency links to download statements. Kinbriva does not request a child-support portal password.

How do I create or restore a backup?

From Casebook, open Family settings and advanced tools, then Encrypted backup and restore. Protect the backup with a separate passphrase of at least 12 characters and keep the passphrase somewhere safe. Restore is available only in an empty workspace.

Is Kinbriva designed for iPhone Duo?

Version 0.2.0 uses adaptive SwiftUI navigation and compact/regular size-class layouts. The outer display uses the compact phone layout and the inner display can use a wider two-column Today view. Final release validation includes open, closed, portrait, landscape, book-style, tabletop and Split View checks in Xcode Device Hub.

Why is recording or transcription unavailable?

Recording requires active Plus access, microphone permission and confirmation that required recording permissions have been obtained. Transcription also requires available on-device speech recognition. Kinbriva does not upload audio as a fallback.

Why can’t I create an on-device summary?

Summary and reply drafts require active Plus access, supported Apple Intelligence hardware and an available on-device model. Manual records and document OCR remain available without the language model. Review every generated statement against the source.

Are exported records automatically redacted?

No. Initials apply only to profile labels. Names and private information may remain in titles, quotations, notes and original files. Review the case report and every attachment before sharing.

How do I delete the local workspace?

From Family settings and advanced tools, open Delete local workspace. Local deletion cannot be undone without a usable backup and does not remove exports, backups, Apple Calendar copies or other people’s copies saved elsewhere.
