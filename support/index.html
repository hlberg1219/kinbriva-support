A little help
with the details.

Find the next step for family setup, original files, payments and backups. These instructions describe the first local native version.

Start with the arrangement.

  1. Set a passcode on the iPhone or iPad, then unlock Kinbriva with device authentication.
  2. Add the applicable parenting order, jurisdiction, time zone and covered children.
  3. Select the order and child before adding a record, amount owed or event.
  4. Keep original files attached, review extracted information and make an encrypted backup.

Common questions

What does Kinbriva Plus cost?

The planned US launch price is $4.99 per month, billed by Apple, with no free trial. The app and subscription are being prepared for Apple review and are not publicly available yet. Plus adds unlimited original-file imports, authorized visit recording, supported local transcription, and supported on-device summary and reply drafts. Each original is limited to 25 MB and documents to 100 pages. Manual organization features and up to 10 original files remain free.

How do I subscribe, restore or cancel?

Open More > Kinbriva Plus. The app displays Apple’s current localized monthly price before purchase. Confirm with Apple to subscribe. The subscription renews automatically each month unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account on another iPhone or iPad. Manage subscription opens Apple’s subscription controls; you can also use Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app or workspace does not cancel a subscription.

After cancellation, expiration or refund, every existing record and original remains available to read, correct, export, back up, restore or delete. Restoring a purchase restores Plus access; use an encrypted backup to transfer local records. Review the Apple Standard EULA and privacy policy.

Can different orders have different parenting roles?

Yes. Each order has its own role and covered children. Support direction is entered separately for each amount owed. Link an existing child profile when adding an amended or replacement order.

How do I add a receipt, order or email?

Open Records and select Add record. Choose the order, child, record type and event date. Import the original file or scan pages with the camera. Supported documents can provide extracted text; other file types can remain attached as originals. Files must be no larger than 25 MB, and a PDF import is limited to 100 pages. EML attachments must be imported separately. Export proprietary or unsupported mail formats as a PDF when needed.

Does a receipt automatically create an amount owed?

No. In Money, add the parent’s portion under the applicable order or agreement, then allocate payments to that obligation. Incoming and outgoing amounts remain separate. Enter the correct share rather than assuming that a receipt total establishes what another parent owes.

How do I import payment records?

Keep the original agency statement under Records. Prepare a reviewed CSV with date, amount and reference columns, using YYYY-MM-DD dates. Choose the amount owed that the rows apply to, inspect the preview and confirm each allocation. Imports are limited to 500 rows per batch. This import does not synchronize the agency account or certify its balance.

Why is recording or transcription unavailable?

Recording and transcription require an active Plus subscription. Recording also needs microphone permission and confirmation that the required permissions for the visit have been obtained. Each clip is limited to one hour and stops when the app becomes inactive. Save it before leaving the form. Transcription also needs speech recognition permission and available on-device language support. If local transcription is unavailable, save the original and add a manual note; the app does not upload the audio as a fallback.

Why can’t I create an on-device summary?

Summary and reply drafts require an active Plus subscription, iOS 26, supported Apple Intelligence hardware and an available local model. Records, manual notes and document OCR work without the language model. Review every proposed statement, name, date and amount against the original, even when the quotation matches extracted text.

How do I use an order when reviewing a message?

Import the complete court order under Records and check the extracted pages against the original. In More, open Confirmed order clauses and save a reviewed exact quotation. Select relevant clauses in Messages and reply drafts. The result provides review questions and an editable reply draft; it does not determine legal compliance or send a message.

Are Gmail, Outlook or support portals connected?

Live mailbox search and automatic child-support portal synchronization are not active in version 0.1.0. Import selected emails or reviewed payment files. Use the official agency site to download statements. Kinbriva does not request a child-support portal password.

Can another parent see my records?

Adding a parent’s name does not grant access. The first native version is a private local workspace with no shared parent accounts or cloud synchronization. Review selected files before deliberately sharing an export through another app.

How do I create or restore a backup?

Open More, then Encrypted backup and restore. Use a passphrase of at least 12 characters, confirm it and save the encrypted backup somewhere accessible outside the app. Keep the passphrase separately; it cannot be recovered. Restore is available only in an empty workspace. Standard automatic device backup of the vault is excluded.

Are exported records automatically redacted?

No. Initials apply only to child profile labels. Names and private information may remain in titles, quotations, notes and original files. Choose the order, child and date range carefully, inspect the PDF and every attachment, and include original files only when intended.

How do I delete the local workspace?

Open More, then Delete local workspace. Follow the typed confirmation and device authentication prompts. Local deletion cannot be undone without a usable backup. It does not remove backups, exports or other people’s copies saved outside Kinbriva.
