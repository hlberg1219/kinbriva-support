# Innexis Support

## Capture a commitment

Use Capture to type a promise or import a supported document or photo. Review extracted details before saving.

## Add evidence

Open a matter and add notes, photos, receipts, or documents so supporting evidence stays connected to the commitment.

## Follow up and verify

Innexis can prepare factual follow-ups. Nothing is sent automatically.

Record partial results, verify the final outcome, and close the matter when the expected outcome actually happens.

## Export a matter

Export a matter when a complete timeline and evidence inventory are needed for personal records.

## Delete data

Delete an individual matter or use the in-app data controls to erase local Innexis data.
