Innexis Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026.

Local-first by design

Innexis 1.0 does not require an account and does not send user-created matters, imported evidence, or extracted commitment details to an Innexis-operated server.

Information stored on the device

Innexis may store notes, dates, monetary amounts, reference numbers, photos, screenshots, documents, source excerpts, timeline entries and outcome records that the user chooses to enter or import.

No advertising or tracking

Version 1.0 contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or data-broker integration.

Notifications and Face ID

Local reminders use Apple's notification system. Optional Face ID or device authentication is handled by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework; Innexis does not receive biometric data.

Exports

Users decide when and where an exported matter record is shared. Once information is shared to another app or service, that destination's privacy practices apply.

Deletion

Users can delete individual matters or erase all local Innexis data from the app's data controls.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics or off-device processing, this policy and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.
