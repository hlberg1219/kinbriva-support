Innexis Support

Capture a commitment

Use the + button to type a promise or import a supported document or photo. Review the extracted details before tracking it.

Add evidence

Open a matter and add notes, photos or documents so the supporting record stays connected to the commitment.

Follow up and verify

Innexis can prepare factual follow-ups. Nothing is sent automatically. Record partial results, verify the final outcome, and close the matter when it actually happens.

Export

Export a matter record when a complete timeline is needed for personal records.

Delete data

Delete an individual matter or use the in-app data controls to erase all local Innexis data.
